One-Time Setup Required

Your device uses a randomized MAC address. This prevents auto-reconnect. Follow these steps once to enjoy seamless WiFi forever:

📱 iPhone / iPad

  • 1
    Go to Settings → Wi-Fi
  • 2
    Tap the ℹ️ next to this network
  • 3
    Turn off "Private Wi-Fi Address"
  • 4
    Tap Rejoin when prompted

🤖 Android

  • 1
    Go to Settings → Wi-Fi
  • 2
    Long-press this network → Modify
  • 3
    Tap Advanced → MAC address type
  • 4
    Select "Use device MAC" and Save

You only need to do this once. After that, your device will auto-connect instantly every time.
