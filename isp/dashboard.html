How do your customers pay, and how do you receive the money?

⚡ Platform will collect payments on your behalf and immediately forward to your preferred method below.

⚡ Customers pay by M-Pesa STK and the money settles instantly into your Equity account. RumaLink never holds your funds — the payer's name and phone appear on your Equity statement.

💸 How IntaSend works

You don't need your own M-Pesa API. Your customers pay into RumaLink's secure IntaSend account, and your earnings build up in your wallet balance. You then withdraw to M-Pesa, a bank, or a paybill/till from the Withdraw page.

Collection fee (deducted when a customer pays): M-Pesa 3%, cards 3.5%, international 4.5%.
Withdrawal fee (deducted when you cash out): M-Pesa KES 100 flat; bank/paybill per IntaSend rates.
To keep fees reasonable, there's a small minimum withdrawal — you'll see the exact fee and what you'll receive before confirming.
